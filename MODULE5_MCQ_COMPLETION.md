## 🎯 MCQ Design Principles

Each MCQ follows pedagogical best practices:

### ✅ Structure
- **Question**: Clear, specific, and technically accurate
- **4 Options**: One correct answer + 3 plausible distractors
- **Correct Answer**: Marked with letter (a, b, c, or d)
- **Explanation**: Detailed explanation of why the answer is correct
- **Incorrect Feedback**: Guidance pointing learners toward the correct concept

### ✅ Content Quality
- **Conceptual Depth**: Questions test understanding, not memorization
- **Real-World Context**: Connects theory to applications (Google supremacy, RSA breaking, drug discovery)
- **Mathematical Rigor**: Includes formulas, complexity classes, and quantitative reasoning
- **Historical Context**: References Nobel Prizes, key researchers, and milestones

### ✅ Examples of Question Types
1. **Formula/Calculation**: "What is the dimensionality of Hilbert space for 50 qubits?"
2. **Conceptual**: "Why are quantum gates reversible?"
3. **Historical**: "Who won the 2022 Nobel Prize in Physics?"
4. **Practical**: "What is the decoherence time for superconducting qubits?"
5. **Comparative**: "Classical vs quantum: Landauer's Principle heat dissipation"
